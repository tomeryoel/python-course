Title: Phishing Email Investigation
Date: 2026-06-19
Reported By: SOC Analyst Team
Affected User: David Cohen
Organization: Amdocs

Incident Description:
A suspicious email was reported by David Cohen from the Finance department.
The email claimed to come from Microsoft Support and requested the user to verify his password using an external login page.

Indicators of Compromise:
Sender Email: support@microsoft-security-check.com
Suspicious Domain: microsoft-login-verification.net
Source IP: 185.23.44.91
Affected Mailbox: david.cohen@company.com

Initial Analysis:
The message appears to be a phishing attempt designed to steal user credentials.
The domain is not associated with Microsoft and was registered recently.
No credential submission was confirmed at this stage.

Recommended Actions:
Block the suspicious domain.
Search for similar emails across all employee mailboxes.
Reset the affected user's password as a precaution.
Notify the Finance department about phishing awareness.
